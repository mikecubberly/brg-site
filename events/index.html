A few useful answers

Before we talk.

Do we need an attendee list?

No. Start with the event and your ideal customers. We can research speakers, exhibitors, partner ecosystems, and available attendance signals. A roster helps, but it is not the only starting point.

Can you work with our sales team?

Yes. BRG can provide research, build the outreach motion alongside your team, or own the agreed outreach and meeting coordination. We define the handoff and reply owners before launch.

How far ahead should we start?

As early as you can. We work backward from the event dates, your available calendars, audience access, and approval process. If the event is close, we prioritize the most useful work that fits the time available.

What counts as a booked meeting?

We agree on the criteria before starting. A confirmed meeting has an agreed time, location or meeting link, and an owner. An invitation sent or a positive reply is tracked separately.

What about travel, staffing, and event logistics?

On-site staffing, event access, travel, venue sourcing, catering, and production are included only when specifically agreed. The plan can focus entirely on pre-event outreach and meeting booking.
