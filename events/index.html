Audience Intelligence

Know who belongs on the list, and why.

Start with your offer, your ideal customer, and the event. We research the audience around that specific context, connecting companies, buying roles, and attendance evidence so your team has more than a list of names.

When it fits: You have an event in mind, but not a reliable audience to work.

What we start with

  • Event URL, location, and dates
  • Target accounts, relevant roles, and exclusions
  • Your offer, existing relationships, and meeting goals

What we build

  • Review speakers, exhibitors, partner ecosystems, and available event signals
  • Check current companies and roles across sources
  • Separate customer fit, contact confidence, and attendance evidence

What you receive

  • Prioritized account and contact research
  • Source notes and an explicit attendance basis
  • Recommended segments and people to validate next

Working for an exhibitor does not prove attendance. Speakers, registrants, likely attendees, and unknowns stay distinguishable.

EventEnricher

Company names aren't a meeting list.

When a partner gives you company names and job titles, finding the actual people can become a research project of its own. We triangulate those fragments through multiple sources and enrichment waterfalls, then review the matches before they reach your team.

When it fits: You have a partial roster, but names, profiles, or contact routes are missing.

What we start with

  • The original roster, with all available fields
  • Your audience criteria and geographic scope
  • Existing contacts and suppression lists

What we build

  • Normalize company names and resolve possible entities
  • Compare titles, current roles, and other available evidence
  • Prioritize stronger matches and flag ambiguous or unmatched rows

What you receive

  • An enriched, deduplicated list with match notes
  • Verified contact routes where available
  • A filterable report or export, with unresolved rows clearly marked

A plausible profile match is not proof that the person registered. We show what supports the match and what still needs confirmation.

Conference
Commanders

Your hunters on the ground

Conference Commanders places people at events with a clear job: find the right prospects, start relevant conversations, and book meetings for your team. The work starts with preparation, so the person working the room understands your offer and knows when to bring your team in.

When it fits: Your team needs dedicated meeting-makers on the floor, not another person waiting at a booth.

What we start with

  • Event access and the agreed on-site coverage
  • Priority people, account briefs, and qualification criteria
  • Messaging, proof points, calendar availability, and handoff owners

What we build

  • Brief the on-site team on the audience and offer
  • Work relevant rooms and conversations around the agreed plan
  • Capture context and route qualified interest to the right person

What you receive

  • Meeting handoffs with conversation notes
  • A record of people reached and agreed next steps
  • Market feedback for your follow-up and next event

Staffing, access, travel, coverage, and follow-up ownership are agreed in scope. A meaningful conversation matters more than an inflated badge-scan count.

Calendars & Hosted Gatherings

Give the right people a reason to show up.

Build an event calendar around the accounts and relationships you want to develop. Connect the larger conference to a more focused dinner, roundtable, partner gathering, or happy hour, with a clear audience and a useful reason to be there.

When it fits: You want the conference investment to create focused conversations beyond the show floor.

What we start with

  • Conference calendar and target markets
  • Hosts, partners, budget boundaries, and capacity
  • The audience and business purpose of the gathering

What we build

  • Prioritize the event opportunities that fit your market
  • Build the guest list, invitation angle, and RSVP process
  • Define host roles, introductions, reminders, and follow-through

What you receive

  • An event and audience plan
  • Guest-list priorities and invitation messaging
  • A host brief, RSVP view, and follow-up responsibilities

Venue sourcing, catering, production, and other logistics are only included when specifically agreed. Invitations and attendance are tracked separately.

Activation & Follow-through

Make the next step obvious.

The audience research is the starting point. We turn it into a practical event motion: who reaches out, what they say, where replies go, how meetings are handed off, and what happens after the conference.

When it fits: You have a useful audience, but need the systems and ownership to put it to work.

What we start with

  • Approved audience and messaging direction
  • Available channels, systems, and sender capacity
  • Reply owners, calendars, and qualification rules

What we build

  • Build audience segments and relevant pre-event invitations
  • Prepare agreed campaign workflows and reply routing
  • Connect event notes to personalized follow-up and CRM ownership

What you receive

  • A campaign or team-ready execution playbook
  • Meeting briefs and clear handoff rules
  • Reporting on replies, meetings, next steps, and opportunity progression

Channels, tools, operating responsibilities, and access are agreed before launch. Results depend on the audience, offer, timing, and execution.
