INVITATION FRAMEWORKReview required

Make the reason to meet clear.

Event context
The named conference and whether the person plans to attend.
Relevant problem
The infrastructure workflow, cost, or operational constraint your offer addresses.
Meeting purpose
A short conversation to establish fit and compare priorities.
Low-friction next step
Agree on a time and a place that work with their conference schedule.

Final copy uses approved claims and prospect-specific evidence.

Existing relationships, exclusions, sender capacity, and reply owners are agreed before launch.

AFTER THE MEETING

Capture what was discussed, the agreed next step, and its owner. Keep the prospect's words in the handoff.
