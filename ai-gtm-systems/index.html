Bottle Rocket Growth

AI agents and GTM systems built around your business.

Client-specific agents and connected workflows with clear jobs, evidence, and human review.

Bottle Rocket Growth (BRG) builds AI agents and workflows around your business, ideal customer profile, and research questions. We start with the work the team needs to do and the decisions it needs to make.

Give each agent a defined job

An agent can help research accounts, cross-check information, monitor relevant changes, or prepare context for a team member. We define the question, permitted sources, output, and review point before expanding the workflow.

The useful output may be an account brief, a source-backed finding, a prioritized item for review, or a prepared handoff. A possible signal still needs interpretation. We keep observed facts, assumptions, and missing evidence distinct.

Connect the steps that matter

  • Research and verification: investigate defined questions and retain inspectable sources.
  • Monitoring and prioritization: surface relevant changes and decide what deserves attention.
  • Workflow handoffs: put context and next actions into the tools the team already uses.
  • Human review: make ownership and approval points clear before consequential actions.

Build around the existing operation

Your systems, data access, budget, and team capacity shape the implementation. We examine where information enters, how it is checked, who acts on it, and how the result is recorded. Sometimes the most useful change is a clear handoff or a simpler process.

Credentials and private information need an appropriate storage and access model. We agree on boundaries for each workflow, including what it can read, prepare, update, or send. The scope determines which integrations and actions are appropriate.

Start with a bounded workflow

A focused first workflow makes it easier to examine output quality and the effort it saves or creates. We review useful findings, errors, missing data, and the team's ability to act on the output before broadening it.

Bring an example of the work you want to improve, the tools involved, and who owns the decision. We can discuss a practical starting point and the review requirements it needs.
