Approved company language

Company boilerplate

Bottle Rocket Growth (BRG) is a Chicago-based, founder-led growth partner for B2B organizations. BRG connects growth strategy, market intelligence, client-specific AI and GTM systems, revenue motions, team building and enablement, and event strategy with hands-on execution. Engagements are built around the client’s market, team, systems, and priorities, with Mike Cubberly leading the relationship and a specialist network supporting the work.

Short description

For listings and introductions

Bottle Rocket Growth helps B2B organizations understand their markets, build practical growth strategies, and put the people and systems in place to execute. The Chicago-based firm combines research, strategy, AI and GTM systems, revenue programs, team enablement, and event work.

Founder bio

Mike Cubberly

Mike Cubberly is the Founder and CEO of Bottle Rocket Growth. He has spent more than a decade working across B2B go-to-market strategy, market research, sales development, team building, and hands-on execution. Mike founded BRG in 2024 and leads client strategy and delivery from Chicago.
