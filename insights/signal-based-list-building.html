Signal-based list building: from web research to useful priorities

Build research-led account and contact lists with evidence, enrichment waterfalls, client-specific AI agents, and clear next actions.

Mike Cubberly · Originally published April 24, 2026 · Revised September 8, 2026

Every client is different. These articles explain approaches we may use, not a checklist applied to every engagement. Research depth, tools, channels, team support, and deliverables depend on the client's goals, market, existing systems, budget, and agreed scope. Examples are not performance guarantees.

A useful lead list explains why an account belongs, which people matter, what is known, and what should happen next. More rows do not necessarily create more opportunity. The goal is research that helps a team make better decisions.

Bottle Rocket Growth (BRG) builds research-led lists and market maps around the client's offer, target market, and growth objectives. That can involve triangulating information across the web, sequencing enrichment providers, and using client-specific AI agents to investigate defined questions. Not every project needs all of those layers.

A signal is evidence, not a verdict

Separate observed facts from interpretations. A new executive, an open role, a product launch, or an announced migration can justify further research. It does not establish budget, urgency, or willingness to speak. Funding does not automatically mean money is available for your service.

Third-party intent also needs a clear level of attribution. Apollo's documentation describes company-level topic research and scoring, with access dependent on the plan. That is not proof that a specific person visited a page or wants a demo. Source: Apollo Buying Intent Overview.

Choose tools for the question

  • Apollo: an example of company and contact research, including buying-intent filters. Verify coverage and available features for the actual subscription.
  • Clay: workflow orchestration and enrichment. Its waterfalls can query providers in a chosen order. A match still needs evaluation; provider agreement is not a guarantee of accuracy. Source: Clay Waterfalls.
  • TheirStack: job and technology research. Read the underlying posting and distinguish when it was published from when it was discovered. Source: TheirStack coverage.
  • Netrows: a B2B data API for sourcing and enrichment, not a reverse-IP visitor-identification service. Source: Netrows.

These are examples, not a required stack or a promise that every engagement uses each product. Existing systems, geography, data rights, coverage, and cost shape the choice.

Build an evidence trail, not just a score

For each useful finding, retain the source link, observed fact, relevant event date, research date, and confidence. Keep the interpretation separate: “job description mentions a migration” is different from “company has approved a migration project.” Deduplicate syndicated announcements so one event does not masquerade as three independent signals.

Set refresh rules around the underlying event. An expired vacancy, a completed transaction, and an upcoming conference need different treatment. There is no universal signal half-life or minimum number of signals that guarantees a good prospect.

Give AI agents bounded jobs

An agent can examine permitted sources, extract evidence, classify it against client-specific rules, and flag conflicts. Require source-backed outputs and an explicit unknown state. Human review matters for consequential claims, uncertain identity matches, and the final decision to act. Do not turn a plausible generated explanation into a fact about a prospect.

Use only data and access routes appropriate for the task. In particular, research workflows should not assume that information visible on LinkedIn may be scraped or automated. Source: LinkedIn's prohibited-software policy.

Connect research to the growth motion

The next action might be an introduction, event invitation, partner conversation, account brief, sales outreach, or no contact yet. Research should also support account planning and market learning, not only message personalization.

Evaluate qualified-account yield, data completeness, research cost, relevant conversations, opportunities, and downstream revenue with clear denominators. Start with a manageable sample and expand when the evidence supports it. Do not promise a conversion lift merely because a list contains signals.
