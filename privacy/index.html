Website inquiries

When you use our contact form, Bottle Rocket Growth (BRG) receives the information you submit, including your name, email address, LinkedIn URL, and message, to respond to your inquiry. The form sends this information to BRG's contact service at clients.bottlerocketgrowth.com.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand visits, page engagement, and successful contact submissions. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes website usage and device information. Our lead event records that an inquiry succeeded; it does not include your name, email, LinkedIn URL, or message. Our website tag removes query strings and fragments from page and referring URLs, and only passes simple campaign labels.

The Google Analytics tag is loaded after you select Allow analytics. You can decline, or change your choice using Analytics settings at the bottom of the page. We store this choice in your browser. Google advertising storage and personalization are disabled in this website tag.

Google's privacy policy explains Google's processing. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites using its services.

Other website services

The homepage also includes a separate RB2B visitor identification integration. The Google Analytics choice controls Google Analytics only. Our pages may use third-party fonts and other website resources, and our hosting provider processes requests to serve and protect the website. External links and third-party services have their own privacy practices.
