A few useful answers

Before
we talk.

Do we need a whole growth rebuild?

Not necessarily. We start with the gap that matters most. That could be a focused ICP and market-mapping project, a team build, an event program, or a broader growth strategy with hands-on execution. LinkedIn outbound is still available when it fits the plan.

Can you build with our team, or help us hire one?

Both. We can work alongside your existing team or help define roles, recruit, onboard, and enable new people. We agree on what BRG owns, what your team owns, and how the work gets handed over.

What do the AI agents actually do?

We build them around each client's ICP and research questions. They can help find and cross-check information, monitor relevant signals, and prepare context for the team. Sources and human review matter. A possible signal is not proof that someone is ready to buy.

What should we bring to the first conversation?

Your offer, your growth goals, who you're trying to reach, and where the work gets stuck. Bring what you know about your pipeline, team, and existing tools. We'll work through the gaps and where BRG can help.
