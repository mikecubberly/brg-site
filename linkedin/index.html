Fit check

Built for teams that need an operator, not another tool.

Good fit

This is for teams that have a specific market to reach but need someone to turn that idea into a disciplined outbound motion.

  • B2B startups with a defined product and unclear repeatable pipeline.
  • Founders who know the market but need sharper targeting and execution.
  • Teams that need borrowed profiles because their own LinkedIn accounts cannot reach enough of the market.
  • Companies with enough ACV for one closed deal to justify a serious outbound test.

What changes

The goal is not more LinkedIn activity. The goal is a managed outbound motion that can be judged, tuned, and repeated.

  • Prospect lists built from true buying signals and visible inclusion logic.
  • Messaging and sequences written around what the buyer is likely to care about now.
  • Day-to-day reply handling, qualification, and meeting follow-through.

Commercial fit

LinkedIn outbound is a better fit when the economics can support a focused campaign, not just light activity. As a working screen, this usually means a product or service with meaningful contract value, a defined buyer, and a budget for several months of iteration.

  • Best fit when average contract value is roughly $50,000 or higher for the standard 5-sender start.
  • Not a strong fit for very low-ticket offers, broad consumer audiences, or teams that cannot support follow-up once replies come in.
