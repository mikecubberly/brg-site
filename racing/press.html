Company boilerplate

Bottle Rocket Racing is a motorsports business development and sponsorship firm based in Chicago, Illinois. Founded in 2024 as the motorsports arm of Bottle Rocket Growth, BRR partners with serious race teams, including NASCAR teams, to build the commercial infrastructure that converts fan enthusiasm into lasting sponsorship revenue.

The firm provides two service lines: Sponsorship Sales Foundation, including sponsor microsites, target-company research, search and AI discovery, and optional simple CRM setup; and Sponsorship Pipeline Generation, offering tiered LinkedIn outreach infrastructure and execution from sender profiles to fully managed campaigns.

Founder bio

Mike Cubberly is the founder of Bottle Rocket Racing and Bottle Rocket Growth. He has spent more than a decade building go-to-market systems for B2B technology companies, with prior roles at Verkada, Glencoco, Primer, and RevGenius. Mike is based in Chicago, Illinois.
