ii. What BRG handles

The Full Outbound Engine

Everything that lives between "we've got an offer" and "a meeting's on the calendar" is on us. You stay focused on what happens inside the meeting room.

01.

ICP & Signal Strategy

We sharpen your ICP, identify the buying signals worth chasing, and build a targeting plan that's narrow enough to be sharp and broad enough to fill pipeline.

02.

List Build & Enrichment

Signal-based prospect lists built through our proprietary system, layering multiple tools, data sources, and enrichment providers. Significantly more valuable than the single-sourced lists most teams run on. Hand-verified, deduplicated, and matched to the messaging variants they'll receive.

03.

Sender Profile Setup

Your sender profiles plug into our LinkedIn automation platform. We configure warmup, daily limits, and connection request pacing so the accounts stay healthy.

04.

Messaging & Sequencing

We write the connection request, the follow-up, and the variants. We iterate on copy weekly based on what's actually moving the needle for your specific ICP.

05.

Live Reply Handling

Inbox monitoring, qualification of warm replies, and meeting handoff. You see qualified conversations only. We keep the noise out of your day.

06.

Reporting & Iteration

A live dashboard at clients.bottlerocketgrowth.com. Weekly review of step rates, copy performance, and target adjustments. No black box.

iii. The Process

From Application to Pipeline

Six steps. The first three are about figuring out whether this is a real fit. The last three are about getting your pipeline moving.

01

Submit Your Application

Tell us about your company, your offer, your ICP, and your sales motion. The form below takes about ten minutes if you know your numbers.

02

Internal Fit Review

We review every application internally. We're looking at TAM, offer maturity, sales motion, and whether we've got the right operator profile to run your campaign well right now.

03

We Reach Back Out

If it's a fit, you'll hear from us directly. If it isn't, we'll tell you why. Either way, you get a real response. No automated rejections, no ghosting.

04

Commission Conversation

Live call. We discuss the commission structure, what triggers payment, the term, and what success looks like for both sides. Numbers get put on paper.

05

We Build Out Your Project

List, messaging, sender setup, infrastructure, and dashboard. The first two weeks are heavy build. We're not running campaigns until the foundation's right.

06

Pipeline Begins

Campaigns go live. Replies and booked meetings start landing in your inbox. We iterate weekly. You sell. We get paid when the pipeline produces.
